Devu Account & Data Deletion

Permanently delete your Devu account and the associated data it owns from inside the mobile app, or request deletion by email if you can no longer sign in.

Delete your account inside the Devu app

  1. Open the Devu mobile app and sign in.
  2. Open More, then Settings.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Complete the two-step confirmation.

Workspace owners may first need to transfer ownership, remove members, or resolve an active subscription before deletion can be completed.

Request deletion without signing in

Email support@devuapp.com from the address associated with your Devu account. Include the account email address and workspace name, if known. We may request additional confirmation to protect the account. Requests are typically processed within 30 days.

Data that is deleted

  • Your Devu account, profile, encrypted credentials, preferences, and workspace membership.
  • Workspaces you own and their clients, practitioners, venues, appointments, notes, custom fields, and uploaded photos.
  • Google and WhatsApp integration records controlled by the deleted account or workspace.
  • Push-notification tokens associated with the account.

Data that may be retained

Operational data is removed or anonymized after a limited recovery and backup cycle. Billing or invoice records may be retained where tax and accounting law requires it. Limited records needed for fraud prevention, security, or dispute resolution may also be retained for the legally required period and are not used for other purposes.

Questions

Contact support@devuapp.com with any question about account or data deletion.
