Devu Support

Help for the Devu mobile app and service.

Contact us

Email support@devuapp.com for help with accounts, appointments, workspaces, notifications, or integrations.

Account deletion

In the mobile app, open Settings and select Delete account. If the request is blocked by workspace members or active billing, follow the on-screen instructions or contact support.

Security

Never send your password, access token, verification code, or complete payment-card information.
