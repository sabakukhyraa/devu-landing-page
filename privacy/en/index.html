Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

Devu is an appointment and client-management service operated by Ali Kerem Ata in Türkiye. This policy explains how the Devu mobile app and service collect, use, share, protect, retain, and delete information.

Information and use

  • Account, profile, workspace, client, practitioner, venue, and appointment information.
  • User-selected photos, push tokens, device details, notification preferences, and technical security logs.
  • Optional WhatsApp and Google identity or Calendar connection data.
  • Information is used to provide Devu, synchronize requested services, protect accounts, diagnose failures, and provide support.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Devu uses contracted infrastructure and integration providers only to deliver the service. Devu does not sell personal data or use the mobile app for cross-app advertising tracking. Data is retained while needed for the service and applicable legal requirements. Users can delete an eligible account from Mobile App > Settings > Delete account or contact support for a privacy request.
